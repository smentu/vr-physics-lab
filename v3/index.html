VR Physics Lab

v3

Your controllers pull two proxy hands through springs with a 200 N force limit. The boxes weigh 1 to 30 kg in a MuJoCo scene that runs on the headset. A box too heavy for one hand stays down; pushing the wall stops at the limit. Every surface is painted procedurally, and contacts make head-tracked binaural sound, best heard on headphones or the headset's speakers.
