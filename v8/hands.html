Hands Lab

v8

The physics lab with your own hands instead of the controllers. The headset tracks 25 joints per hand. Each physics hand follows them: the wrist through the lab's arm spring (200 N limit), the fingers through 21 joints with human-scale strength. The fingers collide, so you hold things by squeezing them, with friction and nothing else. Small objects sit at the front of the table, the 1–30 kg boxes where they were.

leaving
