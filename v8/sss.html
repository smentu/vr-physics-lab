Translucency Room

v8

A dark room with one lamp you can pick up. On the table are four translucent media (wax, jade, gummy, skin) as a sphere, a cube and a cylinder, and a low-poly Stanford bunny. Seven cheap subsurface-scattering shaders run on the headset; the trigger switches between them. Hold the lamp behind an object to see light come through it. The spring-hand physics lab is here. Stanford bunny courtesy of the Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory.
